About Me

I'm a sophomore in Semiconductor IC Design at FPT University, focused on RTL design and verification. I keep each project small enough to finish properly: a spec with port tables first, a Python golden model, a self-checking testbench, then synthesis and timing results I can explain. Lately I've been comparing architectures that compute the same thing, such as an adder tree against a systolic array, to see where area and timing actually go.
